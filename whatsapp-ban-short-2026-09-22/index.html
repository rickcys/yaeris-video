opted in. WhatsApp tracks a
rolling seven-day quality score
built from blocks,
spam reports, and engagement.

new signal, messages nobody replies
to now count against you,
too. Enough of that,
and your rating drops from
green to yellow to red.
Red comes with real messaging
limits.

say.
