different ways to treat that
listing after approval, and most
builders only ever do one.
The submit once half.
Filled in at launch,
approved, never touched again,
slowly drifting out of date
as the actual project changes
underneath it.
The Maintained Half. The
Creator dashboard lets you update
at any time, and your
current live listing stays visible
to everyone searching while a
bigger update goes through review,
so you're never offline.
The tell is simple.
Check the last time your
own listing was actually edited,
against the last time the
project itself meaningfully changed.
It's free, it never expires,
and nothing about editing it
requires starting over or going
dark while a change is
reviewed.
