## Full script (verbatim, 8 lines)

1. "Is Crawl API worth it" depends on whether the sites you're actually targeting fight back or not.
2. There are two fundamentally different answers, and the deciding factor is your targets, not the tool.
3. Not a fit: simple static pages with no real bot protection. A basic fetch call is already enough, no reason to reach for more.
4. A real fit: sites with genuine bot detection or rate limiting, and a need for both a REST endpoint in your backend and MCP access for an AI agent, from the same key.
5. The test is three questions. Do your targets have real bot protection? Do you need both REST and MCP access? Can you validate your actual use case inside 100 free credits before paying anything?
6. That validation step exists specifically so you're not guessing -- 100 free credits, no card required, test against your real targets first.
7. The fit question here was never really about the API. It's about what you're actually trying to scrape.
