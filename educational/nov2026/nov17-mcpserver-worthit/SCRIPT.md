"Is MCP Server worth it" depends on how many tools your agent actually needs to reach, not on the protocol itself.

There are two fundamentally different answers, and scale is what decides it.

## Line 3 -- Who it's not for
Not a fit: one agent, exactly one tool. A direct integration is genuinely simpler, don't overthink that case.

## Line 4 -- Who it is for
A real fit: multiple tools, and you don't want to own the integration maintenance for each one yourself, forever.

## Line 5 -- The test
The test is three questions. Does your agent need more than one or two external tools? Do you want that integration maintained for you long-term? Does pay-per-connection, not per task, actually fit how often it gets used?

## Line 6 -- Grounding
The account itself is free, one OAuth 2.1 login unlocks all 134 tools once you're in, and you only pay for what you actually connect.

## Line 7 -- Reframe / close
The fit question here was never really about MCP as a standard. It's about how many integrations you're willing to own yourself.
