## Storyline (7 beats + follow CTA)
1. Hook -- adding a new lead-gen tool usually means ripping out the CRM you already trust.
2. The rip-and-replace fear: new tool, new system of record.
3. The actual pattern: Radar handles finding+first-contact, results export to your existing CRM.
4. The test -- check whether a tool asks to replace your CRM or just feed it.
5. Grounding -- SERP Lookup + Crawler + Outreach, one account, exports to wherever you already track pipeline.
6. Reframe -- the gap it fills sits in front of your CRM, not instead of it.
7. Close + follow CTA.
