Adding a new lead-gen tool
usually sounds like it means
ripping out the CRM your
team already trusts.
Usually it doesn't have to.
There are two fundamentally
different ways a new tool
can slot into an existing
stack, and the difference matters
a lot to whoever has
to actually migrate anything.
The fear. Rip and replace,
a brand new system of
record, months of retraining everyone
on a different interface.
The actual pattern here.
Radar handles finding leads
and making first contact,
and the results export
to whatever CRM you're
already running pipeline through.
The test is simple, check
whether a given tool is
asking to replace your system
of record, or just asking
to feed it.
SERP Lookup, the Crawler, and Outreach
all run from one account,
and nothing about that requires
abandoning the CRM
your team already knows.
The gap this actually fills
sits in front of your
CRM, not instead of
it — the part between
finding a lead and
getting that lead into
your pipeline in the first place.
